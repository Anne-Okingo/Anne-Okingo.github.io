My Bio

Alice Anne Awuor Okingo

Alice Anne Awuor Okingo is a Kenyan software developer and data scientist with three years of experience applying technology, data science, and machine learning to solve complex problems and drive social impact. She specializes in building data-driven, scalable solutions that amplify the voices of underrepresented women and girls, while also delivering robust software applications across sectors.

Currently a Software Developer at Zone 01 Kisumu, Anne founded Heal-Impact in 2024, providing psychosocial support for GBV survivors and using data insights to inform gender prevention initiatives. She previously served as Project Manager at the Institute for Faith and Gender Empowerment (2019–2024), leading programs that strengthened women’s civic participation. As Project Manager for Code Ssipers, she mentors young developers and created Afyakuu, an AI-driven tool for cancer prediction in resource-limited settings.

Her work combines software engineering, machine learning, and data analysis to create impactful, scalable solutions that advance SDGs 5, 10, 16, and 17, empowering communities, supporting evidence-based decision-making, and delivering measurable social impact.
